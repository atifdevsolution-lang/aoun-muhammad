## 📊 Data Analytics & Data Entry Projects Showcase

### 1. Sales & Revenue Performance Dashboard
* **Domain:** Data Analytics & Visualization
* **Tools Used:** Power BI, MS Excel
* **Overview:** Transformed raw e-commerce sales data into an interactive visual dashboard tracking monthly revenue trends, regional performance, and top-selling product categories.
* **Key Outcome:** Provided actionable business insights that identified key revenue drivers and profit margins.

### 2. E-Commerce Inventory & Stock Tracking Management System
* **Domain:** Inventory Management & Data Entry Automation
* **Tools Used:** Google Sheets, Advanced Formulas (`QUERY`, `XLOOKUP`), Conditional Formatting
* **Overview:** Built an automated stock tracking spreadsheet with real-time stock balance calculations, automated low-stock re-order highlights, and detailed supplier records.
* **Key Outcome:** Eliminated manual inventory entry errors and reduced tracking time significantly.

### 3. Customer Churn & Retention Analysis
* **Domain:** Advanced Data Analysis
* **Tools Used:** Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Jupyter Notebook
* **Overview:** Conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on subscription service data to identify root causes behind customer cancellation and churn trends.
* **Key Outcome:** Highlighted retention bottlenecks and recommended strategic data-driven retention solutions.

### 4. PDF & Unstructured Data Extraction & Database Cleaning
* **Domain:** Data Cleaning & Data Entry Processing
* **Tools Used:** Python, MS Excel, Power Query
* **Overview:** Extracted unformatted text and operational table data from PDF files, cleaned and standardized the datasets, and structured them into ready-to-use Excel databases.
* **Key Outcome:** Streamlined raw data ingestion into clean, verified business records.

### 5. Financial & Expense Optimization Report
* **Domain:** Business Analytics & Financial Data Entry
* **Tools Used:** MS Excel (Pivot Tables, Data Models, Financial Formulas)
* **Overview:** Designed a budget variance and expense tracking model for small-to-medium business operations, mapping quarterly spend against projected targets.
* **Key Outcome:** Visualized cost-cutting opportunities and budget allocation efficiency.
